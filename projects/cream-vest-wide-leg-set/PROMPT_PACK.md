## Cara guna dalam Google Flow

1. **Frames to Video** → **Veo 3.1** (Quality) → **9:16**.
2. Upload `model-front.png` sebagai **First frame**.
3. Paste prompt. Jana 3–4 versi, pilih yang muka dan baju paling konsisten.

## Prompt

```
8-second vertical 9:16 video, one continuous shot, starting exactly from the
uploaded first frame. A polished but natural fashion video for TikTok in a
bright, elegant room, shot on a phone on a tripod, full body framing at the
start. The camera is steady; it makes only a very slow, subtle push-in during
the last 3 seconds. No cuts.

Keep EVERYTHING exactly as in the first frame. The same young woman, same
face, same skin, same makeup. She wears a light cream hijab (soft chiffon
shawl draped neatly over the chest), a fitted white long-sleeve inner top,
and the PRODUCT, a matching cream set, unchanged in every frame:
- Vest: cream-beige woven cotton-blend sleeveless vest, V-neck, three
  buttons down the front, princess seams from chest to waist, a matching
  fabric belt tied in a bow at the waist with two long ties hanging down, a
  longer back hem with pleated side panels.
- Pants: cream high-waisted wide-leg trousers with front pleats and side
  pockets, falling straight and full to the floor over pointed cream shoes.
Accessories: small cream structured top-handle handbag with a gold clasp in
her right hand, thin gold watch.

Setting, identical in every frame: beige panelled wall, a brass floor lamp
with a white shade glowing warmly on the left, white sheer curtains on the
right, a beige carpet with a soft wave pattern.

0–3 s: She stands elegantly in the first-frame pose and starts talking with a
soft smile and natural expressions, looking at the camera. Her free hand
lightly touches the waist belt bow.
3–5 s: She does a slight turn of her hips to the side so the vest's longer
back hem, the side pleats and the wide-leg drape show, then turns back to
the front.
5–8 s: She walks two calm, confident steps forward toward the camera, the
wide legs swaying. The shot moves in slowly to a waist-up framing. Warm
golden sunlight now streams through the sheer curtains on the right and
falls on her: it lights the cream vest, so the woven texture, the princess
seams, the buttons and the belt bow are clearly visible, and the soft sheen
of the trousers' fabric glows. Soft light and shadows play on the pleats. She
ends with a gentle smile, holding the pose.

Dialogue: she speaks in natural Malaysian Malay with a soft, warm, confident
young female voice and a Malaysian accent, looking at the camera, speaking
with natural lip-sync. She says:
"Selesa pakai baju ni, nampak cantik dan elegan."

Audio: only her voice, recorded naturally, with light room tone. No
background music, no sound effects.

Real fabric physics: light woven cotton, wide trousers sway softly, the
hijab stays neatly in place. Photorealistic, natural skin texture, no beauty
filter, no slow motion, no cinematic grading, no text, no subtitles, no
watermark.

Avoid: changing face, different woman, hijab changing style or colour,
hijab blowing away or revealing hair, distorted hands, extra fingers,
outfit changing colour, white or grey outfit, missing buttons, missing belt
bow, missing vest, sleeves on the vest, skinny pants, jeans, skirt,
handbag changing or disappearing, background change, flickering, morphing,
jump cut, subtitles, text, music, English speech.
```

## Jika ada masalah

| Masalah | Penyelesaian |
|---|---|
| Dialog tak sempat habis | Dialog sudah pendek; jika perlu, buang `nampak`: `"Selesa pakai baju ni, cantik dan elegan."` |
| Muka berubah masa melangkah | Kurangkan gerakan: ganti `two calm steps` dengan `one calm step` |
| Tudung berubah atau terbuka | Ulang `the hijab stays exactly the same, neatly draped, hair fully covered` |
| Cahaya terlalu terang, kain putih | Tambah `exposure balanced, cream colour and fabric texture fully visible` |
| Tali pinggang / butang hilang | Tambah `the belt bow and three buttons stay visible` |
| Loghat Indonesia | Tambah `Malaysian Malay accent, not Indonesian` |
