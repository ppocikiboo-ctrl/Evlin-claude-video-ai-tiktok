## Style

- **Proof-first hook:** the video opens on the real used cotton pads with **TENGOK APA YANG TERANGKAT 😳**. A magnifier lens zooms 1.9× into the pad. This is the real footage enlarged, not retouched.
- **Word-by-word captions in the CapCut style:** white type with a black outline, written in first person as if the creator is talking. The newest word turns pink, and keywords get a neon highlighter swipe (pink or lime).
- **STEP chips:** each one is timed to the real action. STEP 1 *Basahkan kapas* sits on the pad pressed on the pump. STEP 2 *Tekap seketika* sits on the pads held on both cheeks. STEP 3 *Lap perlahan* sits on the eye wipes.
- **RATING AKU 💯 card:** this is the creator's own rating. Five stars fill on each row: Tanggal makeup, X pedas, Tak berminyak, Mudah guna.
- **Transitions and beat:** cuts are whip-pans (horizontal smear and slide), and the picture zooms slightly on every kick at 104 BPM. The music bed is Am–F–C–G.
- **CTA:** **NAK CUBA? 😍**, then **TEKAN BEG KUNING 🛍️** and *tengok harga hari ni*, with 👇 and pulse rings pointing at the product link.
