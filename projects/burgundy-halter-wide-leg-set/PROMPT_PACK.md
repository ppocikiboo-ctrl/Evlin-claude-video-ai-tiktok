# Burgundy Halter Top + Wide-Leg Pants Set — Google Flow OOTD, 8s, bercakap

Mirror selfie OOTD 8 saat. Perempuan memperagakan set baju dan seluar burgundy,
memegang baju dan seluar dengan tangan bebas sambil bercakap dalam Bahasa
Melayu. Telefon menutup muka sepanjang video.

**Gambar rujukan:** [`reference/model-mirror-selfie.png`](reference/model-mirror-selfie.png)

## Cara guna dalam Google Flow

1. **New project** → mod **Frames to Video** → model **Veo 3.1** (Quality).
2. Upload `model-mirror-selfie.png` sebagai **First frame**. Aspect ratio **9:16**.
3. Paste prompt di bawah. Jana 2–4 versi, pilih yang set baju paling tepat.

## Prompt

```

uploaded first frame. An authentic TikTok OOTD mirror selfie filmed on a phone
in a bedroom. Real user-generated content, not an advert: handheld, mostly
steady with very slight natural hand movement, warm afternoon sunlight with
soft light stripes on the floor, phone auto-exposure. No cuts, no zoom, no
camera moves other than her hand.

Keep EVERYTHING exactly as in the first frame. The same young Malaysian woman:
very long dark-brown wavy hair, half-up with a small bun on top, slim natural
body, real skin texture. She holds the same burgundy phone with a matching
case and a triple camera in front of her face, in the same hand as in the
first frame, for the WHOLE video. The phone always covers her face; her face
is never revealed. The black structured leather handbag stays hanging on the
```

## Jika ada masalah

| Masalah | Penyelesaian |
|---|---|
| Ayat terpotong / terlalu laju | Pendekkan: `"Cantiknya baju ni! Nampak elegan. Jalan-jalan, event, office pun sesuai."` |
| Loghat Indonesia | Tambah `Malaysian Malay accent, not Indonesian` |
| Warna jadi merah terang | Tambah `deep burgundy wine-red, not bright red` di awal dan hujung |
| Beg tangan hilang / berubah | Ulang `the black handbag stays on her forearm the whole time` |
| Tangan rosak semasa pegang seluar | Buang tarikan seluar di 4–6 s, ganti dengan `she smooths the trouser leg` |
