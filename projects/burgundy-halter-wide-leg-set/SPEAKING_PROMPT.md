## Jika ada masalah

| Masalah | Penyelesaian |

| Loghat Indonesia | Tambah `Malaysian Malay accent, not Indonesian` |
| Warna jadi merah terang | Tambah `deep burgundy wine-red, not bright red` |
| Beg tangan hilang | Ulang `the black handbag stays on her forearm the whole time` |

| Tangan rosak masa sentuh seluar | Buang bahagian seluar, ganti dengan `she shifts her weight slightly` |
