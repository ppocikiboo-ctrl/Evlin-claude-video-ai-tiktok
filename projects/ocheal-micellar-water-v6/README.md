# O'CHEAL Micellar Water — v6 "luxury editorial" (second edit of the v5 take)

This is a second edit of the same talking-head take as [v5](../ocheal-micellar-water-v5/README.md).
It reuses v5's cut and processed voice (`../ocheal-micellar-water-v5/build/base.mp4` and
`voice.wav`), so run v5's `build_base.py` first, then `python3 render.py` here.

Output: `output/ocheal_micellar_tiktok_v6.mp4`. It is 27.6 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, about −15 LUFS, about 67 MB.

## What is different from v5

- **Look:** plum glass panels with champagne-gold borders and serif type revealed letter by letter, a warm tint, vignette and film grain. Every jump cut gets a gold light streak and an RGB split, and cuts settle by pulling out instead of punching in. The product tail is 5 s instead of 2.6 s.
- **Sound effects (new set):** shimmer riser, glass tap, sub impact, camera shutter, type ticks, water drop and a "ka-ching" on the buy button.
- **Music:** a slower R&B-style bed at 96 BPM (Dm–Bb–C–Am), ducked under her voice.

## Selling structure

**Hook (problem + consequence):** *Tidur dengan makeup?*, underlined, then *esok pagi kulit rasa berat & kusam 😩*, then *ni cara aku tanggalkan makeup 👇*.

**FAB cards (Feature → Advantage → Benefit)**, each timed to what she shows:

| # | Feature | Advantage | Benefit | On screen while she… |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hyaluronic Acid + Rose | Angkat makeup tanpa gosok kuat | Tak pedih, kulit terasa lembut | brings the bottles up |
| 02 | Formula tak berminyak | Tak melekit lepas lap | Terus sambung skincare seterusnya | handles the bottle |
| 03 | Botol pam 300ML | Tekan sekali, kapas terus basah | Rutin malam cepat & kemas | pumps onto the pad and wipes |

**Result line:** *hasilnya: kulit terasa lembut & segar 🤍*

**CTA formula** (one beat at a time, with progress dots):
1. **KENAPA** (why click): *Sisa makeup buat kulit rasa berat*
2. **TINDAKAN** (action): *Tekan beg kuning, checkout O’CHEAL*
3. **KENAPA SEKARANG** (why now): *Malam ni pun kau nak tanggal makeup 🌙*
4. **HASILNYA** (benefit): *Esok bangun, kulit terasa lembut ✨*
5. **KALAU TAK** (cost of not acting): *Malam ni tidur dengan sisa makeup lagi 😩*
6. Product name, then the pulsing **TEKAN BEG KUNING 🛍️** button with 👇.

The "why now" reason is the viewer's own routine tonight, so no stock or discount urgency is invented.
Benefits are phrased as feel ("terasa"), and claims match the brand slides (gentle, non-greasy, no dryness, Hyaluronic Acid + Rose, 300 ML pump).
