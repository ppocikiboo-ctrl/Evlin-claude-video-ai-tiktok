# Yellow Gingham Tie-Front Top — Google Flow OOTD Mirror Selfie (8 s)

Mirror selfie OOTD 8 saat. Perempuan memperagakan top gingham kuning dengan
lapisan putih berfril. Telefon menutup muka sepanjang video. Senyap secara
asal (letak lagu sendiri); versi bercakap ada di bawah.

**Gambar rujukan:** [`reference/model-mirror-selfie.png`](reference/model-mirror-selfie.png)

## Butiran baju (dari gambar)

| Bahagian | Perincian |
|---|---|
| Top luar | Kuning pastel, kain gingham (petak kecil) bertekstur ringan, tali bahu nipis dengan tepi fril, tepi leher berfril |
| Depan | Terbuka di tengah, diikat reben kecil dengan tali nipis di bawah dada, dikumpul (gathered) di bawah dada lalu kembang seperti babydoll |
| Lapisan dalam | Atas putih berlace eyelet di tepi leher, butang putih di depan, hem putih berlapis-lapis sifon nipis yang kelihatan di bawah top kuning |
| Pakaian lain | Skirt pleated mini hitam, stoking putih, sneakers putih tebal, gelang perak nipis, beg hitam di bahu |

## Cara guna dalam Google Flow

1. **Frames to Video** → **Veo 3.1** (Quality) → **9:16**.
2. Upload `model-mirror-selfie.png` sebagai **First frame**.
3. Paste prompt. Jana 3–4 versi, pilih yang corak gingham dan lapisan putih paling tepat.

## Prompt (senyap)

```
8-second vertical 9:16 video, one continuous shot, starting exactly from the
uploaded first frame. An authentic TikTok OOTD mirror selfie filmed on a
phone in a bedroom. Real user-generated content, not an advert: handheld,
mostly steady with very slight natural hand movement, soft warm indoor light,
phone auto-exposure. No cuts, no zoom, no camera moves other than her hand.

Keep EVERYTHING exactly as in the first frame. The same young woman: very
long dark-brown hair, half-up with a small bun on top, slim natural body,
real skin texture. She holds the same burgundy phone with a triple camera in
front of her face with the same hand for the WHOLE video, so her face is
never revealed. Black pleated mini skirt, white crew socks, chunky white
sneakers, thin silver bracelet, black shoulder bag.

THE PRODUCT, unchanged in every frame: a pastel yellow gingham (tiny check)
camisole-style top with thin straps and a frilled edge along the neckline and
straps. The front is open in the centre and tied with a small ribbon bow just
below the bust with thin ties; it is gathered under the bust and flares out
like a babydoll. Underneath it shows a white layer: a white eyelet-lace
trimmed neckline, a row of white buttons down the centre, and a hem of sheer
white chiffon ruffles in two tiers that peek out below the yellow top.

0–2 s: She stands relaxed in front of the mirror, weight on one leg. With her
free hand she lightly touches the small bow below her bust.
2–4 s: She gently tugs one tie of the bow, then lifts the edge of the white
chiffon ruffles and lets them fall, so the layered look is clear.
4–6 s: She turns her hips slightly left, then right; the babydoll flare of
the yellow top and the white ruffles swing softly.
6–8 s: She turns back to the front, tucks a strand of hair, and ends in a
cute, relaxed OOTD pose with the top clearly visible.

Real fabric physics: light gingham cotton and sheer chiffon move softly and
settle. The gingham check stays small and consistent. Natural hair movement.
Photorealistic, no beauty filter, no slow motion, no cinematic grading.
SILENT VIDEO: no music, no speech, no sound effects. No text, no subtitles,
no watermark.

Avoid: revealing her face, phone moving away from her face, changing face,
different woman, distorted hands, extra fingers, phone changing colour,
warped mirror, top changing colour, bright yellow or green top, plain
yellow without check, large checks, missing bow, missing white layer,
missing ruffles, closed front, sleeves, long top, skirt changing, background
change, flickering, morphing, jump cut, music, voice.
```

## Versi bercakap (pilihan)

Dalam prompt di atas, buang baris `SILENT VIDEO...` dan tambah:

```
Dialogue: she speaks in casual Malaysian Malay with a cute, warm young
female voice and a Malaysian accent, voice coming from behind the phone:
"Comelnya top ni! Warna kuning lembut, ada lapisan frill putih. Padan dengan
skirt hitam terus nampak manis."
Audio: only her voice, light room tone. No music, no sound effects.
```

Jika ayat terpotong: `"Comelnya top ni! Kuning lembut, ada frill putih. Nampak manis!"`

## Jika ada masalah

| Masalah | Penyelesaian |
|---|---|
| Corak gingham hilang / jadi kuning rata | Tambah `tiny yellow and white gingham check clearly visible` |
| Lapisan putih hilang | Tambah `white buttoned layer and white chiffon ruffles always visible below the yellow top` |
| Reben hilang | Tambah `the small tied bow stays below the bust` |
| Muka terdedah | Ulang `the phone covers her face the whole time` |
| Veo masih letak bunyi | CapCut Volume 0, atau `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -an -c:v copy out.mp4` |
