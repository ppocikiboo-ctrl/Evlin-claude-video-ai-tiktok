# O'CHEAL Micellar Water 300ML — TikTok edit v2 ("sticker pop")

This is a second take of the same product with a new UGC clip and a different
graphic style. The pipeline is the same as [v1](../ocheal-micellar-water/README.md):
`build_base.py` → `track.py` → `render.py`. The text helpers, sprites and synth
engine are loaded from `../ocheal-micellar-water/render.py`, so keep both folders together.

Output: `output/ocheal_micellar_tiktok_v2.mp4`. It is 20.1 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps, −14 LUFS.

## Style compared with v1

| v1 (soft K-beauty) | v2 (sticker pop, made for affiliate selling) |
|---|---|
| frosted-glass cards, serif titles | torn tape strips + die-cut stickers with hard shadows |
| soft fade/scale entrances | slam-in with overshoot + screen shake, slide-out |
| leader line + ring on the cap | yellow scribble loop + hand-drawn arrow on the tracked bottle, 300ML burst badge |
| 3-step how-to panel | animated ✅ checklist (KENAPA SUKA?) |
| pink light-leak cuts | pink/yellow stripe-wipe transitions + white flash cuts |
| 116 BPM D major bed | 126 BPM F major bed; thud and marker-scribble SFX |

## Claims

- "X PEDAS" and "kulit terasa lembut" come from the creator's own experience, so they are phrased as feel ("terasa"), not as a guaranteed result.
- "X berminyak" and "x rasa kering" are backed by slide 7 of the brand images ("refreshing and non-greasy", "no fear of dryness").
- "Mata & bibir" is backed by slide 7 ("gentle removal of eye makeup and lip balm").
- The used pads are shown as they are, with no "look how dirty" claim. Like v1, there is no acne claim, no before/after, and no BUY 1 TAKE 1 unless it is confirmed.
