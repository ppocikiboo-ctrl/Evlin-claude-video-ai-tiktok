## Jika ada masalah

| Masalah | Penyelesaian |
|---|---|
| Tak jadi bergerak ke tingkap | Pecahkan jadi dua klip: klip 1 depan di cermin; klip 2 mula dengan `she is already standing at a sunny window, close-up from chin to waist` |
| Kain putih terang / hilang detail | Tambah `exposure balanced, fabric weave and red colour fully visible` |
| Kain nampak seperti satin | Tambah `matte cotton poplin, not satin` |
| Muka terdedah semasa pusing | Ulang `the phone covers her face during the turn` |
| Reben di belakang hilang | Guna Pilihan A dan pastikan gambar belakang diupload |
