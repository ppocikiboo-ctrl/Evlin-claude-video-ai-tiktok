# Brown Cargo Mini Skirt — Google Flow (Veo) OOTD Mirror Selfie, 8s, bercakap

Video OOTD mirror selfie 8 saat. Perempuan dalam gambar rujukan memperagakan
skirt cargo mini coklat sambil bercakap dalam Bahasa Melayu. Telefon menutup
muka sepanjang video.

**Gambar rujukan:** [`reference/model-mirror-selfie.png`](reference/model-mirror-selfie.png)

## Cara guna dalam Google Flow

1. Buka Google Flow → **New project**.
2. Pilih mod **Frames to Video**, model **Veo 3.1** (Quality untuk hasil terbaik).
3. Upload `model-mirror-selfie.png` sebagai **First frame** (start frame).
4. Aspect ratio **9:16 (portrait)**.
5. Paste prompt di bawah (dalam bahasa Inggeris, Veo lebih ikut arahan dalam
   bahasa Inggeris). Dialog tetap dalam Bahasa Melayu.
6. Jana 2–4 versi, pilih yang skirt paling tepat dan suara paling natural.

## Prompt (paste semua)

```
8-second vertical 9:16 video, one continuous shot, starting exactly from the
uploaded first frame. An authentic TikTok OOTD mirror selfie filmed on a phone
in a bedroom. Real user-generated content, not an advert: handheld, mostly
steady with very slight natural hand movement, soft warm indoor light, phone
auto-exposure. No cuts, no zoom, no camera moves other than her hand.

Keep EVERYTHING exactly as in the first frame. The same young Malaysian woman:
very long dark-brown wavy hair, half-up with a small messy bun on top, slim
natural body, real skin texture. She holds the same burgundy phone with a
glossy case and a triple camera in front of her face with her right hand for
the WHOLE video. The phone always covers her face; her face is never revealed.
Thin gold bracelet on her free wrist.

Outfit, unchanged in every frame:
- Top: dark chocolate-brown fitted cropped polo tee, short sleeves, soft
  ribbed knit, flat collar with an open V-neck placket and no buttons, ending
  just above the skirt waistband so a little waist shows.
- THE PRODUCT, skirt: dark chocolate-brown cotton twill cargo mini skirt,
  high waist, belt loops, one silver metal shank button and a zip fly at
  centre front, two large rectangular patch pockets with button-less flaps on
  the front, tonal topstitching, a short side slit at each hem, slightly
  A-line, mid-thigh length. Same colour, same shape, same pockets, same
  length as the first frame.
- Shoes: clean chunky white low-top sneakers.

0–2 s: She stands relaxed in front of the full-length mirror, weight on one
leg, and starts talking. With her free hand she lightly smooths the front of
the skirt and touches the pocket flap.
2–5 s: She shifts her weight and turns her hips slightly left, then right, so
the mirror shows the skirt's front pockets, the side slit and the A-line
shape. Her free hand rests on the side of the skirt.
5–8 s: She turns back to the front in a casual, confident OOTD pose, gives a
small happy nod behind the phone, and taps the skirt pocket once.

Dialogue: she speaks in casual Malaysian Malay with a natural, friendly, warm
young female voice and a Malaysian accent, like talking to her TikTok
followers. Her voice comes from behind the phone. She says:
"Cantik skirt ni! Sesuai pakai pergi jalan-jalan, dan untuk OOTD. Quality pun
macam skirt mahal tau."

Audio: only her voice, recorded naturally by the phone, with light room tone.
No background music, no sound effects.

Background stays identical: beige floor-to-ceiling wardrobe on the left,
dark-wood door with a silver lever handle on the right, white quilted bed,
small white side table, light glossy tiled floor with soft reflections, beige
rug corner, wooden mirror frame on the edges. The mirror reflection is
physically correct.

Photorealistic, real cotton twill fabric that is firm and structured and
moves only slightly. Natural hair movement. No beauty filter, no slow motion,
no cinematic grading, no text, no subtitles, no watermark.
```

## Negative prompt

Flow tidak ada kotak negative prompt. Tambah baris ini di hujung prompt jika
hasilnya lari:

```
Avoid: revealing her face, phone moving away from her face, changing face,
different woman, distorted hands, extra fingers, phone changing colour,
floating phone, warped mirror, skirt changing colour, black skirt, khaki
skirt, denim skirt, pleated skirt, long skirt, trousers, shorts, missing
pockets, extra pockets, pockets moving, missing button, changed top, white
top, sleeveless top, background change, flickering, morphing, jump cut,
subtitles, captions, text, music, English speech.
```

## Jika ada masalah

| Masalah | Penyelesaian |
|---|---|
| Ayat terpotong / terlalu laju | Pendekkan: `"Cantik skirt ni! Sesuai untuk jalan-jalan dan OOTD. Quality macam skirt mahal."` |
| Suara keluar dalam loghat Indonesia | Tambah `Malaysian Malay accent, not Indonesian` |
| Muka terdedah | Ulang ayat `The phone always covers her face` di awal dan hujung prompt |
| Poket skirt berubah | Kurangkan gerakan: buang pusingan pinggul di 2–5 s, ganti dengan `she shifts her weight slightly` |
| Ada sari kata dalam video | Tambah `no on-screen text of any kind` |
