# O'CHEAL Micellar Water — v7 "aqua countdown" (third edit of the v5 take)

This is a third edit of the same talking-head take as [v5](../ocheal-micellar-water-v5/README.md)
and [v6](../ocheal-micellar-water-v6/README.md). It reuses v5's cut and processed voice
(`../ocheal-micellar-water-v5/build/base.mp4` and `voice.wav`). To build it, run v5's
`build_base.py` first, then `python3 render.py` here.

Output: `output/ocheal_micellar_tiktok_v7.mp4`. It is 26.2 s, 1080×1920 and 30 fps, mixed to −14 LUFS.

## Format: "3 SEBAB" countdown

A listicle countdown keeps people watching until number 1. Each reason is timed to what she shows on camera.

| Out | On screen | Footage |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.9 s | Big water-filled **3** + **SEBAB AKU SAYANG / MICELLAR WATER NI 💧** | talking |
| 3.2–6.3 s | Speech bubble *tengok sampai habis 👀* (retention prompt) | talking |
| bottles up | **SEBAB #3**: *Ada Hyaluronic Acid + Rose 🌹*, *bantu kekalkan kelembapan kulit* | bottles brought up |
| handling | **SEBAB #2**: *Tak berminyak, tak melekit*, *lepas lap, kulit rasa ringan* | handling the bottle |
| pump + wipe | **SEBAB #1**: *Tak pedih langsung 🙌*, *pam atas kapas, lap lembut-lembut* | pad on the pump, first wipe |
| talking | Speech bubble *hasilnya? kulit terasa lembut & segar 🤍* | talking |
| bottles held up | **BONUS**: *botol pam 300ML, senang & kemas 💧* | bottles held up |
| tail (3.6 s) | *O’CHEAL Micellar Water 300ML*, **NAK KULIT TERASA LEMBUT? 😍**, a yellow **TEKAN BEG KUNING 🛍️** button with water sloshing inside, 👇, and *🔖 simpan video ni untuk rutin malam kau* (save prompt) | frozen last frame, slow push-in |

## Look and sound

- **Countdown numerals** (Baloo 2) fill with rising water that has a moving wavy surface and a foam line. Ripple rings spread from each numeral.
- **Panels** are frosted bubble panels and speech bubbles with an aqua rim, set in Fredoka. Bubbles rise throughout.
- **Grade and camera:** a fresh cool grade with lifted aqua shadows and slightly richer pinks. The camera drifts slowly and bounces on the beat.
- **Cuts** use a "water rises" wipe: the new shot comes up from the bottom behind a wavy, foamy waterline.
- **SFX:** bubble plop, drip, sonar bloop, splash and a water whoosh, plus a chime on the CTA.
- **Music:** a 122 BPM bed (Cmaj7–Am7–Fmaj7–G), ducked under her voice.

## Claims

Benefits come from the brand slides (Hyaluronic Acid + Rose, moisture, non-greasy, gentle, 300 ML pump) or are phrased as feel ("terasa", "rasa"). There are no price, stock or discount claims.
