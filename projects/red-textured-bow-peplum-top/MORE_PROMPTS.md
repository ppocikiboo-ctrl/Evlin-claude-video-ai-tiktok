# Red Textured Bow Peplum Top — Prompt tambahan (Google Flow, 8 s)

Guna gambar rujukan yang sama dalam `reference/` (`front-view.png`,
`back-view.png`). Semua prompt ini berkongsi blok **tetap** di bawah. Salin
blok ini ke dalam setiap prompt supaya baju dan model kekal sama.

```
Keep EVERYTHING exactly as in the reference images. The same young woman:
very long dark-brown wavy hair, half-up in a small bun, slim natural body,
real skin texture. She holds the same burgundy phone with a triple camera
raised in front of her face with the same hand the WHOLE video, so her face
is never revealed. Black pleated mini skirt, white crew socks, chunky white
sneakers, thin silver bracelet, small black shoulder bag.

THE PRODUCT, unchanged in every frame: a deep crimson-red top in textured
jacquard-like fabric with a subtle embossed floral swirl pattern. Front:
sweetheart neckline, a large soft bow at the centre of the bust with two long
tails falling down the front, thin shoulder straps, fitted waist, flared
peplum with a wavy scalloped hem. Back: wide shoulder straps, a square open
back, fitted body, and a double-layered peplum with a wavy scalloped hem.
```

Baris **Avoid** biasa untuk semua: `revealing her face, phone moving away from
her face, changing face, distorted hands, extra fingers, phone changing
colour, warped mirror, top changing colour, pink or orange top, plain smooth
fabric, missing bow, missing peplum, background change, flickering, morphing,
jump cut`.

## Prompt 3 — Bercakap Bahasa Melayu (depan, 8 s)

**Flow:** Frames to Video → `front-view.png` sebagai First frame → 9:16.

```
8-second vertical 9:16 video, one continuous shot, starting exactly from the
uploaded first frame. An authentic TikTok OOTD mirror selfie filmed on a
phone in a bedroom, handheld but steady, warm soft indoor light with sunlight
from the side. No cuts, no zoom.

[BLOK TETAP]

0–2 s: She stands relaxed in front of the mirror, weight on one leg, and
starts talking. Her free hand touches the big bow.
2–5 s: She lifts the edge of the peplum with her fingertips and lets it fall,
then turns her hips slightly left and right so the scalloped hem swings.
5–8 s: She turns back to the front, a small nod behind the phone, and ends
in a sweet OOTD pose.

Dialogue: she speaks in casual Malaysian Malay with a natural, warm young
female voice and a Malaysian accent, like talking to her TikTok followers.
Her voice comes from behind the phone. She says:
"Cantiknya top ni! Reben besar kat depan, kain pun bertekstur nampak mahal.
Sesuai pakai jalan-jalan dan OOTD."

Audio: only her voice recorded naturally by the phone, light room tone. No
music, no sound effects. Photorealistic, no beauty filter, no slow motion,
no text, no subtitles, no watermark.
```

Jika ayat terpotong: `"Cantiknya top ni! Reben besar, kain nampak mahal. Sesuai OOTD."`

```
No cuts, no zoom.

[BLOK TETAP]

She does a light, happy joget-style dance to an imagined upbeat beat,

0–3 s: Facing the mirror, she sways her hips side to side and bounces her

peplum swings with her.
3–4 s: Her free hand sweeps her long hair over her front shoulder.
4–6 s: She turns around slowly on the beat in small dancing steps, phone
still raised in front of her face.
6–8 s: With her back to the mirror she keeps swaying so the double
scalloped peplum swings and the open back and wide straps show. She ends
matching the last frame.

SILENT VIDEO: no music, no speech, no sound effects. Photorealistic, no
beauty filter, no slow motion, no text, no subtitles, no watermark.

```

## Prompt 6 — Showcase tangan sahaja, tanpa muka dan badan (8 s)

Sesuai sebagai B-roll. Tiada model, jadi hanya baju yang kelihatan. Guna mod
**Ingredients to Video** dengan kedua-dua gambar sebagai rujukan baju.

```
8-second vertical 9:16 video, one continuous handheld phone shot in a bright
bedroom. No face and no body: only a woman's hands (long nude-pink almond
nails, thin silver bracelet) holding up the exact deep crimson-red textured
top from the reference images by its straps, in front of a beige wardrobe.
Warm golden-hour sunlight from a window rakes across the fabric so the
embossed floral swirl texture and the wavy scalloped hem are clearly visible.

0–3 s: She holds the top up by the straps; it hangs open, showing the
sweetheart neckline, the large bow with two long tails and the flared peplum.
3–5 s: She smooths the fabric with one hand, then lifts the scalloped hem
into the sunbeam.
5–8 s: She turns the top around to show the wide straps, open square back
and double scalloped peplum, then turns it back to the front bow.

Real fabric physics, slightly crinkled textured fabric, no studio lighting.
SILENT VIDEO: no music, no speech, no sound effects. No text, no subtitles,
no watermark.

Avoid: face, mannequin, hanger, flat lay, plain smooth fabric, pink or orange
colour shift, missing bow, extra fingers, distorted hands, flickering,
morphing, jump cut.
```
